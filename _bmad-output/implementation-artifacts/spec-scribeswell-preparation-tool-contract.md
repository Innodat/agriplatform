## Intent

**Problem:** Local preparation has a written owner handoff and native test-only rehearsal, but no closed request contract or total execution/COMMIT/cleanup policy for a future preparation tool. The rehearsal does not verify live owner evidence or bound a complete invocation.

**Approach:** Specify an app-owned versioned preparation request, separate private target and trusted owner admission, fixed supervision bounds and truthful outcome/reconciliation rules. Limit the first executable foundation to a pure parser, leaving supervised disposable qualification and live owner integration as later bounded deliveries. No runtime implementation, actor/role/grant preparation, migrations, database/image/provider operations, Access/KDG/Leave/factory changes or activation. Preserve standalone signup and agreed UX.
