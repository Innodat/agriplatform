## Corrections required before consolidation is complete

1. **Policy overview still excludes daily earning and offers a removed setting.** `apps/leave/docs/features.md:258–272` says “Annual/anniversary front-loading or monthly accrual” and conditionally shows “rounding when prorated.” This contradicts ADR-0090 and ADR-0103 and the same document's later detailed rules. Minimal fix: replace the mode bullet with “Annual entitlement available daily, upfront, or in monthly instalments; manual-only grants remain separate”; describe fixed precision rather than configurable rounding; remove the rounding conditional from the policy form sentence.

2. **The confirmed-decision table still requires calendar-only monthly timing and omits daily mode.** `apps/leave/docs/implementation-plan.md:91,93,108,124`: initial grants resume “next month,” accrual dates use first/last calendar day, and MVP modes omit daily earning. Minimal fix: make initial grant dates relative to the aligned instalment period; describe entitlement-period basis independently of daily/upfront/monthly availability; monthly dates are policy-selected start/end of each leave-year-aligned instalment under ADR-0099/0100; list daily/upfront/monthly annual-target modes plus separate manual grants under ADR-0090. “Month-end availability” can be retitled “End-of-instalment availability” while keeping start-of-effective-day usability.

3. **The balance delivery gate still implies posted automatic accrual and transaction-only balances.** `apps/leave/docs/implementation-plan.md:558–568` requires “accrual ... entries” and says “rerunning jobs creates no duplicates” with balances reproducible “from transactions.” `apps/leave/docs/features.md:2439` likewise specifies “accrual jobs.” These conflict with ADR-0106 if interpreted literally. Minimal fix: distinguish immutable actual events from derived automatic earning/expiry/carry-over effects and snapshots; gate deterministic replay on effective-dated inputs plus actual events, repeated reads with no duplicate earning, and retry-safe actual mutations. Replace the NFR bullet with “Idempotent commands and notification delivery; deterministic repeated automatic-entitlement calculation.” Do not require an accrual job or stored current balance.

4. **Older acceptance examples retain scheduled-posting language.** `features.md:737–744,763,1301–1302` describe “retrying” scheduled grants and link only to pre-pivot ADRs. Minimal fix: say repeated calculation never double-counts the same automatic earning portion, while retrying an actual manual/correction command remains idempotent; link ADR-0090/0106 in the common acceptance paragraph. Retain date/grant terminology where it describes entitlement becoming available rather than persistence.

5. **Monthly rounding qualifications remain contradictory/ambiguous.** `features.md:610–612` says prorated monthly rounding “remains separate”; `features.md:750` says aligned partial periods retain “final-grant rounding”; nearby later material correctly applies ADR-0105 cumulative precision. Minimal fix: explicitly distinguish standalone upfront grants from related monthly portions; monthly partial periods and annual-rate changes preserve cumulative fractions under ADR-0105. The standalone 481-minute example should be labelled an upfront standalone grant so it cannot be read as independently flooring every partial monthly instalment.
