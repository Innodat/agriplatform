Look especially at the explanation below the acknowledgement, the balance figures, and the amount of scrolling. A larger scale is a tradeoff: easier-to-read small text, with more vertical space.

16px is a design starting point, not a universal accessibility requirement. W3C guidance requires text resizing to 200% without lost content or function. WCAG 2.2 AA target-size guidance specifies 24 by 24 CSS pixels, subject to exceptions; our 44–48px control heights aim for more comfortable use.

The proposed type sizes use relative units. These static studies do not establish compliance: real controls, responsive transitions, zoom and keyboard interaction still need browser verification. The desktop study remains horizontally scrollable on narrow screens so its composition can be examined; the mobile sample illustrates the intended narrow layout.
