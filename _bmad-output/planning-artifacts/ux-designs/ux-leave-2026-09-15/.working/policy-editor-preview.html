Backdating, overlaps and corrections

Backdated requests follow normal review. Same-day requests warn about overlap and cannot exceed the employee’s scheduled working hours in total.

Approved date or duration corrections cancel the original and submit a linked replacement for fresh approval. Changing this policy does not silently change existing requests.
